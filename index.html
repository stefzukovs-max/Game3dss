NINE HUNDRED STEPS TO THE CROSS

TONIGHT

Morro do Cruzeiro. Four thousand people up a granite slope. One road reaches the bottom — above it there is nothing but the Escadão and the lanes that branch off it.

The transmitter on the water tower carries the Saturday baile, and it carries the lookouts: when a patrol turns off the coast road the music cuts and a voice says which gate.

The eviction notices are served Monday. The Battalion is coming up tonight to take the tower first. The hill has until dawn.

Fiction. Every character, crew, unit and location in this game is invented. Stylised, non-realistic depiction — no real people, organisations or places.
